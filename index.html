Hi, I'm Oleh Front-End Developer

I turn designs into working interfaces. I enjoy polishing a project until every detail sits right — a habit I picked up as a CNC operator, where precision is not optional. Self-taught and currently moving into front-end development, looking for my first developer role.

About

I spent years as a CNC operator, where a single wrong value ruins the part. That taught me to check things carefully and finish what I start — habits that turned out to matter more in development than I expected.

Over the past year I moved from occasional experiments with code to complete, deployed projects. I taught myself — mostly from documentation, video courses and a lot of trial and error. Along the way I learned to structure data for the task at hand, separate logic from markup, handle errors and edge cases, and set up an automated build and deployment pipeline.

I don't memorise syntax, I look things up. What I do bring is the habit of seeing a task through until it actually works and every detail sits right.
